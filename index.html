About Anmol Cloth House

Hosiery & knitted apparel from Baheri, Bareilly

Built for Wholesale Partners

Anmol Cloth House and Anmol Textile Industries manufacture hosiery and knitted apparel in bulk for stockists, distributors and brands, with a focus on consistent quality, dependable delivery and direct factory pricing.

Leadership
Tasleem Ahmad, Founder
Amaan Choudhary, Managing Director

Why Partner With Us?

  • Direct factory rates: no middleman margins.
  • Pan-India shipping: routes covering Kanpur, Delhi, Jaipur, Patna and beyond.
  • Quality checks: fabric GSM testing, colour-fastness and trim inspection.

Our Factory & Machinery

Gursauli-Baheri Highway, Baheri, Bareilly — knitting, cutting, printing and stitching under one roof

High-Speed Circular Knitting Machines

Seamless tubular fabric for tees, polos, hoodies, fleece and thermal knits.

Optitex CAD Pattern Cutting

Digital pattern making, grading and marker planning for consistent sizing in every bulk lot.

Automated Carousel Screen Printing

Multi-colour prints on panels and finished garments, built for repeat bulk runs.

4-Needle Flatlock Stitching Lines

Strong, flat, stretchable seams for sportswear, joggers, tracksuits and activewear.

Automatic Buttonhole & Button Stitching

Clean automatic buttonholes and button attaching for polos, shirts and uniforms.
